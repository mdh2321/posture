Tips for better posture

Monitor position

Position your monitor at arm's length, with the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level.

Keyboard & mouse

Keep your keyboard and mouse close to avoid reaching. Wrists should be neutral, not bent up or down.

Chair height

Adjust your chair so feet are flat on the floor and knees are at a 90-degree angle.

Take breaks

Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Sitting posture

Sit back in your chair with your back supported. Avoid leaning forward or slouching.

Phone usage

Hold your phone at eye level instead of looking down to avoid "text neck" and tension headaches.
