# Privacy Policy — Aligned (Posture and Movement Reminders)

_Last updated: June 16, 2026_

Aligned does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What the extension stores

All data stays in your browser, managed by Chrome:

- **Settings** (reminder intervals, working hours, sound and theme preferences) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so Chrome can sync them between your own signed-in browsers.
- **Usage counters** (how many posture checks and breaks occurred each day, kept for 30 days) and the list of recently shown stretches are saved with `chrome.storage.local` on your device only.

## What the extension does not do

- No data is sent to any server — the extension makes no network requests.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
- No access to your browsing history, tabs, or page content.
- The idle permission is used only to ask Chrome "is the user active right now?" so reminders don't fire while you're away. Nothing about your activity is recorded.

## Data removal

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
